# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary user is the author. He comes back to his own notes, books and study plan, and the garden is first of all his working tool.

An outside reader is welcome and nothing is tuned for them at the author's expense. Who that reader is has not been decided.

## Product Purpose

A personal wiki and a journal on `sanch.pet`, under the name "Учусь мыслить" (learning to think): evergreen notes that grow by revision, dated posts, and modules around them (a library, a course catalogue, a tech radar, a race calendar).

The author named four things as success, with no order between them:

- he thinks better: writing into the garden makes his thinking clearer, and the corpus grows by revision;
- he is understood: a reader who walks through the garden sees what he is busy with, what he reads and how he reasons;
- the texts are read and discussed;
- the garden becomes a platform: the modules add up to a tool one can use, and later to something others can build on.

## Positioning

A garage with the door open. A text is written in the garden from its first line and stays out of the site until published; the repository of texts is public. The garden shows work in progress on one person's thinking, with its sources and its reading list beside it, where a blog would show finished pieces.

## Operating Context

- Texts are written in Obsidian and published by pushing the texts repository; the site is built from it by a Quartz v5 fork.
- A new text is announced in the author's Telegram channels.
- A text names its sources; a source card (a book, a course) lists the texts that name it.
- The race calendar draws its data from a separate repository that is refreshed daily.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Reading works without a script.** Content is reachable with JavaScript off: the list under the radar, the picks under the calendar. A drawing adds to the content and never replaces it.
- **Two languages in one build.** Russian comes first, English mirrors it where a translation exists, and a translation lives at the matching path.
- Only real, non-draft notes are published. Private notes and drafts never reach the site or its index.
- Not established as commitments: whether the site stays free of third-party analytics, and whether a published address is permanent.

## Brand Commitments

- The name "Учусь мыслить" and the address `sanch.pet`.
- The mark: a red valve handwheel with an eye for a hub, from the author's hand sketch (`brand/`).
- Post covers are black and white, with grain.

## Evidence on Hand

In the texts repository today: 23 book cards and one course card, two tools on the radar, the race calendar, one published evergreen note in each language and one published post. Drafts exist beside them. There are no testimonials, reader numbers or press, and none may be invented.

## Product Principles

1. The author's own use decides. Where the author's use and an outside reader's convenience pull apart, the author's use wins.
2. A text is never finished; the garden shows its revisions instead of hiding them.
3. A module is something the author uses himself before it is something shown.
4. Content first: whatever is drawn, the content under it stays reachable as plain text.
