# Notes for agents

- The look is specified in `DESIGN.md`; read the section you touch before changing anything a reader sees.
- Upstream Quartz comes in as patches only, never as a merge (README, Upstream).
- Before a PR: `npm test`, `npm run check` and a local build with the texts (README, Running locally).
- Credit what you bring in. A change that adds a technique, an open-source component, a library, a font or a borrowed idea that readers meet on the site also adds it to the colophon in [sanchpet/garden-texts](https://github.com/sanchpet/garden-texts) (`ru/colophon.md` and `en/colophon.md`): what it is, a link to the original or its source, and its author. The change is not done without that line. Build tooling a reader never meets stays out.
